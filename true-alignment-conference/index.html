About the Conference

Where Relationships Find
Their Deepest Purpose

The True Alignment Conference is a gathering for all who desire to understand God's beautiful design for relationships — whether you are single, married, or in any season of life. We come together to learn, be restored, and discover how the Gospel is the foundation of every flourishing relationship.

1 Thessalonians 5:23

The Theme Explained

Wholesomeness — God's
Restorative Nature

God's design is to make all things in alignment with His nature — which is Good. When something is broken, His nature moves not to condemn, but to restore, heal, and realign.

"The nature of sin makes and keeps things broken. The nature of God makes things whole."

Wholesomeness is not naïve optimism — it is the theological reality that Christ came to restore what sin fractured. In Him, our relationships, identities, and purpose are made complete.

This conference is an invitation to step into that completeness — spirit, soul, and body — together.

Meet the Conveners

Dare & Titi Med

Dare & Titi Med Conveners · Rock & Root Initiative

Dare and Titi Med are devoted followers of Jesus Christ and have been married for over 13 years. Their commitment to the ministry of the Gospel began during their university years, and since then they have served in various capacities — as support pastors, teachers, counselors, and ministry leaders.

They are deeply passionate about strengthening families, marriages, and relationships. Together, they lead SittingWithTheMeds, a platform for marriage coaching and mentoring, and host Worship, Chat & Chill — a Bible-study-centred community for honest conversations about faith. Dare and Titi are blessed with two wonderful children and are currently pursuing a Master's degree in Apologetics in the United States.

Dare Med
Technology & Innovation · Founder

A technology and innovation enthusiast with over 20 years of experience in the IT industry. Dare currently serves as a Principal Enterprise Architect at a global nonprofit in Canada. He holds a BSc from Ghana and an MBA from the University of South Wales, and is the founder of MyNamely and Brillare I.S., and co-founder of Milky Express.

Titi Med
IBCLC · Founder & CEO, Milky Express

An International Board Certified Lactation Consultant and founder of Milky Express — dedicated to supporting breastfeeding mothers through products, counseling, and education. Inspired by her own journey after becoming a mother in 2015, Titi has championed maternal health across Nigeria and Africa. She holds a degree in Economics from KNUST and continues to empower mothers with knowledge and support.
